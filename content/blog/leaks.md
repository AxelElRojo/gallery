As the title says (and as you can see below), most of the pictures I shot using Lucky SHD 400 on my F601 have light leaks, I am not sure why it happened but I have a theory.

{{< embed-photo roll="lucky400_f601" src="29.JPG" caption="FML." >}}

The F601 has a small window that lets you see what film stock is inside the camera at any given time (and if the camera is empty, I suppose). Lucky films come in plastic canisters instead of metal canisters which are used by most manufacturers (all others as far as I'm aware), **if** the canisters are not only plastic but also *slightly* smaller, it would mean the window light seal is not making proper contact with the canister and therefore not blocking light, causing weirdly consistent leaks.

Do I have proof? No. Can I test my claim and therefore get proof? Yes, but that would mean taking my digital calipers out of my tool drawer and I don't feel like doing that. I guess the below could be considered evidence, it's Fujicolor 400 shot on the same F601 and none of the pictures have those evil light leaks:

{{< embed-photo roll="fuji400_fanfest" src="1.JPG" caption="Is my composition terrible? Maybe. Is that the point? No." >}}
